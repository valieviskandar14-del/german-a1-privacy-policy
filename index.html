German A1 Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

The app does not require an account and does not send lesson results to our servers. Your optional name, selected language, unlocked lessons, and scores are stored on your device. You can delete them by clearing the app storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app.

Advertising

The app displays ads using Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google’s SDK may collect or share the advertising ID, App Set ID, app and ad interactions, IP address, and diagnostic information to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. Data in transit is protected with TLS. You can manage advertising privacy choices using “Privacy options” in the app. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

Speech

German pronunciation uses the text-to-speech service on your device. Data handling depends on the TTS provider configured on your device.

Retention and contact

Your name and learning progress are stored only on your device and are not stored on our servers. Advertising data is handled under Google’s policies. For questions about this policy, contact valieviskandar14@gmail.com.
